Claims requiring correction

  • Valuation: remove unsupported 1.5–3× market range and 3–6 month validity. Avoid automatic add-backs for replacement labor or recurring maintenance. Preserve an example only with clearly stated illustrative assumptions.
  • Numerical records requests: 24 months or two years may be framed as a suggested preparation range, not a universal legal requirement. No fixed diligence duration or six-figure savings claim without evidence.
  • Buying/selling: remove unsourced price ranges, financing percentages, total closing periods, and 20–40% price-loss assertion. Do not treat a letter of intent as universally binding.
  • Licensing: separate ABC and local authorization, conditional temporary permits, and case-specific timing/fees. CUB does not simply travel with a state license.
  • Leases: read the actual transfer, consent, options and guaranty language; remove automatic release and blanket landlord-right assumptions. Remove unsupported assignment-fee and consent-time ranges.
  • Health and accessibility: distinguish ownership changes from remodel scope and jurisdiction; no automatic permit transfer or blanket ADA grandfather exemption.
  • Local markets: remove unsupported rent rankings, demand claims, exclusivity and license premiums. Use named areas as places to investigate, not universal concept recommendations.
  • Blog listing widgets: frozen “Available Now” listings/prices are unrelated to article intent and lack live availability evidence. Recommend replacing these interruptions with contextual informational links, leaving listing pages intact.
