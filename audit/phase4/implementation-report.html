4. Overlap resolution

The Help due-diligence guide owns the complete checklist query because its existing procedural context and workstream granularity fit that job. Blog now owns interpreting findings and deciding whether to proceed, renegotiate or pause. It links prominently to the Help checklist; Help links back for decision interpretation. Financial, lease, permit, equipment and specialist-escalation material remains represented across these complementary resources.

Both URLs remain indexed and self-canonical. Their previous duplicate checklist targeting was a structural risk, not an observed ranking result. Without authority evidence, preservation and differentiation are the least destructive supported actions. Type 41/47 comparison versus ABC transfer procedure, second-generation premises versus lease assignment, and valuation education versus seller representation also retain distinct owners. Beverly Hills content now explains local transaction considerations rather than competing as a broker service page.
