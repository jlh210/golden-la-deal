LA Local Market Guides

Restaurant Broker in Beverly Hills

5 min readUpdated July 24, 2026By Richard Stein, COO
Key Takeaways
  • Beverly Hills restaurant rents typically run $8–$18 psf/month NNN on prime corridors.
  • Type 47 liquor licenses in 90210 command premiums well above LA County averages.
  • The Beverly Hills Planning Commission actively reviews new hours, entertainment, and outdoor dining.
  • Off-market opportunities dominate — very few Beverly Hills restaurants publicly list.

The Beverly Hills market

Beverly Hills is one of the highest-priced and most tightly regulated restaurant markets in Los Angeles County. Rents on the Rodeo, Canon, and Beverly corridors reflect luxury retail demand. Landlords are selective, the Planning Commission is active, and the ABC treats new hours or entertainment as a full public process.

Where transactions actually happen

  • The Golden Triangle (Rodeo / Canon / Beverly) — luxury operators only.
  • South Beverly Drive — neighborhood dining, higher deal velocity.
  • Beverly Boulevard corridor — mix of casual and fine dining.
  • Wilshire — larger footprints, hotel-adjacent concepts.

What Beverly Hills buyers get wrong

  • Underestimating the landlord's qualification bar — Beverly Hills landlords vet operators harder than most of LA.
  • Assuming a valid CUB from another city transfers — it doesn't; Beverly Hills CUBs are location-specific.
  • Ignoring valet, sign, and outdoor-dining approvals — each is a separate city process.
  • Not budgeting for the higher food-cost customer expectation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about la local market guides.

What does a Beverly Hills restaurant lease cost?+

Prime Beverly Hills restaurant rents run roughly $8–$18 per square foot per month NNN in 2026, with the Golden Triangle at the top of the range. Secondary streets and Beverly Boulevard trade lower.

How much is a Type 47 liquor license in Beverly Hills?+

License premiums in 90210 vary with scarcity but typically run well above the LA County average because the ABC has issued a limited number of Type 47s in the city and new-issuance is slow.

Do I need Beverly Hills city approvals in addition to state ABC?+

Yes. Beverly Hills reviews outdoor dining, entertainment, extended hours, and any exterior signage as separate city processes. Build these into your timeline.
