Primary-source checks support a more careful regulatory presentation: ABC temporary permits depend on eligibility and departmental approval, rather than automatic permission (ABC temporary permit conditions). A restaurant valuation considers documented earnings, the assets included in the sale, remaining lease term and assignment conditions, equipment condition, and the status of licenses and permits. Draft answer example — What should a restaurant buyer prepare? A restaurant buyer should define a budget, preferred location, concept and operating requirements, then organize evidence of available funds and relevant experience. For a specific opportunity, the buyer should review financial records, lease terms, equipment ownership, and license and permit status. Penthouse Advisors helps buyers evaluate these details and coordinate the next steps with the parties involved. Link: Restaurant acquisition advisory → buyer page.
